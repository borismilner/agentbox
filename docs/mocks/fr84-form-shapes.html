FR84 - a form card whose options you could not read

Three design questions went out as one ask_user_form. Every choice field clipped its options mid-word at about 38 characters, and the fields sat below the fold, so the card opened on prose with no controls in sight. It was answerable only because the body spelled the options out and the recommended one arrived preselected. Your words: "we'll have to think of a better visual approach". Below is today, then three approaches - the mock cards are live, so click them.
